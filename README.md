# Kakuma Skills & Jobs Hub

### Bridging the Gap Between Education and Employment

**Learn Skills. Find Opportunities. Build Your Future.**

## 1. Project Overview

Kakuma Skills & Jobs Hub is a digital platform designed to address youth unemployment in Kakuma Refugee Camp and the surrounding host community in Turkana County, Kenya.

Many educated young people remain unemployed because they lack practical skills, workplace experience, professional networks, and access to reliable job opportunities.

Our platform aims to bridge this gap by connecting young people with practical skills training, mentorship, internships, entrepreneurship opportunities, and available employment opportunities.

The project promotes equal access to education, skills development, and decent work for both refugees and members of the host community.

## 2. Problem Statement

Despite having academic qualifications, many young people in Kakuma face challenges transitioning from education into employment.

The major challenges include:

- Limited access to practical and technical skills training.
- High youth unemployment.
- Lack of workplace experience.
- Limited access to job and internship opportunities.
- Inadequate career guidance and mentorship.
- Limited professional networks.
- Difficulty accessing entrepreneurship and business support.

These challenges prevent many educated young people from becoming economically independent.

## 3. Our Solution

Kakuma Skills & Jobs Hub provides a centralized digital platform where young people can discover learning opportunities, develop practical skills, connect with mentors, and access employment opportunities.

The platform aims to transform education into practical skills and practical skills into employment.

## 4. Main Features

### A. User Registration and Profiles
- Registration for young people, employers, mentors, and training organisations.
- Personal profiles.
- CV upload functionality.

### B. Skills Development
- Digital and IT skills.
- Technical and vocational training.
- Entrepreneurship and business management.
- Communication and teamwork.
- Workplace readiness.

### C. Job and Internship Opportunities
- Job vacancy listings.
- Internship opportunities.
- Apprenticeship opportunities.
- Job search and filtering.
- Online application support.

### D. Mentorship and Career Guidance
- Connect young people with professionals.
- CV-writing assistance.
- Interview preparation.
- Career development guidance.

### E. Entrepreneurship Support
- Business development information.
- Financial literacy.
- Self-employment opportunities.
- Access to entrepreneurship training and support.

### F. Dashboard
- View available opportunities.
- Track job applications.
- Discover recommended training.
- Receive opportunity notifications.

## 5. Target Users

Our platform targets:

1. Unemployed educated youth in Kakuma Refugee Camp.
2. Young people from the host community.
3. Refugees seeking employment opportunities.
4. Employers and local businesses.
5. NGOs and training institutions.
6. Mentors and career professionals.
7. Young entrepreneurs.

## 6. Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

### SDG 4: Quality Education

The project supports inclusive and equitable quality education by improving access to practical skills, digital learning, vocational training, and lifelong learning opportunities.

### SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

The project promotes productive employment, entrepreneurship, economic inclusion, and access to decent work opportunities for young people.

## 7. Expected Impact

Kakuma Skills & Jobs Hub aims to:

- Reduce barriers to youth employment.
- Improve practical and digital skills.
- Increase access to internships and job opportunities.
- Promote entrepreneurship and self-employment.
- Strengthen connections between employers and job seekers.
- Support economic inclusion of refugees and host communities.
- Encourage young people to become self-reliant.

## 8. Technology and Development

The platform can be developed using:

- HTML5 – Website structure.
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive design.
- JavaScript – Interactive functionality.
- Backend technology – User authentication and application management.
- Database – Secure storage of user profiles, jobs, and applications.

The final technology stack will depend on the implementation requirements.

## 9. Project Sustainability

The platform will pursue sustainability through:

- Partnerships with NGOs and development organisations.
- Collaboration with local employers and businesses.
- Partnerships with vocational training institutions.
- Grants and innovation funding.
- Corporate social responsibility partnerships.
- Sponsored training programmes.

Basic access for young job seekers should remain affordable or free.

## 10. Future Improvements

Future development plans include:

- Full user authentication and secure accounts.
- Employer and training-provider dashboards.
- CV upload and application tracking.
- Verified job listings.
- Online learning resources.
- Mobile application development.
- SMS notifications for users with limited internet access.
- Multilingual support for the Kakuma community.

## 11. Current Development Status

The project begins as a website prototype demonstrating its main concept and user experience.

The next development phase will focus on building a complete platform with a secure database, user accounts, verified opportunities, and real application functionality.

**Note:** Job and training examples in the prototype are demonstrations, not verified live vacancies.

## 12. Vision

Our vision is to create a community where every educated young person has an opportunity to develop practical skills, access decent employment, and contribute positively to society.

We believe that education should not end with a certificate. It should open doors to opportunities, independence, and a better future.
